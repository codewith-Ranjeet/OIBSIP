About

Ratan Naval Tata is an Indian industrialist and philanthropist, who served as the chairman of Tata Group, one of India's largest and oldest conglomerates. He is known for his humility, vision, and dedication to nation-building. His contributions span industries, innovation, and humanitarian efforts.

Legacy

  • Transformed Tata Group into a global brand.
  • Launched iconic projects like Tata Nano & Tata Indica.
  • Major contributions to charity, education, and healthcare.
  • Recipient of Padma Vibhushan and Padma Bhushan awards.
